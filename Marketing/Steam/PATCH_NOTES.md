# Loop 9 — Patch notes

Player-facing text for the Steam "Post an announcement" box. Newest first.
Keep hidden achievements and anomaly mechanics vague: the point is to tell
players something changed, not to hand them the answer sheet. Internal names
(`Watcher`, `Creep`, `ringing floor`) stay out of the public copy.

---

## v1.0.7 — 11 September 2026

**New**
- Two new anomalies have moved into the office. One of them would rather you didn't look. The other is patient.
- Some desk phones now ring. Whether you pick up is your business. Whether you should is another matter.
- Four new achievements, all hidden. You'll know when you earn them.
- The main menu has a "Report a bug" button in the bottom-right corner. Use it — every report goes straight to us.

**Changes**
- Anomalies now rotate more. A type you just dealt with is much less likely to show up again for the next few floors, so a single run sees more of the roster.
- The two most common anomaly types were showing up more often than intended and have been brought back in line with the rest.
- New sound design for the new anomalies, including the moment the lights go out.
- Text anomalies have been reprinted: the wrong words now look like they were always part of the page rather than pasted on.
- Credits updated with the sources for the new audio.

**Fixes**
- Answering a ringing phone no longer turns the floor's lights back on. It only gives you the lift back. The dark is yours to keep until the next floor.
- Trying to reply on a line that has gone dead now actually tells you it's dead, instead of doing nothing.
- The caller on a ringing phone is no longer labelled as Dragojlo. It isn't him.
- Fixed a case where a moving object's attached parts stayed behind.
- Fixed two garbled strings in the chat.

**For the curious**
- All new text is localised in English, Serbian, German, French and Russian.
